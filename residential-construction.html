Residential Services

BUILDING HOMES WITH QUALITY, INTEGRITY, AND CRAFTSMANSHIP

At our Residential Construction division, we specialize in delivering high-quality homes that reflect the lifestyle, vision, and needs of every homeowner. As part of a full-service general contracting firm, we combine technical expertise, skilled craftsmanship, and strong project management to ensure every residential project is completed safely, efficiently, and to the highest standards.

From new custom homes to renovations, additions, and structural improvements, our team is committed to creating living spaces that are beautiful, durable, and built to last.

Custom Homes

CUSTOM HOME CONSTRUCTION

Building a home from the ground up is one of the most important investments a homeowner can make. Our team works closely with clients, architects, and engineers to bring your vision to life.

Our custom home services include:

  • Site preparation and foundation construction
  • Structural framing and roofing systems
  • Electrical, plumbing, and mechanical installations
  • Interior and exterior finishes
  • Energy-efficient building systems
  • Final inspections and occupancy preparation

We manage every stage of the construction process to ensure quality workmanship, schedule control, and cost transparency.

Renovations

HOME RENOVATIONS

Whether you want to modernize your home or restore an older structure, our renovation services improve functionality, comfort, and value.

Common renovation projects include:

  • Kitchen remodeling
  • Bathroom upgrades
  • Whole-home renovations
  • Structural repairs and reinforcement
  • Interior redesign and finishing
  • Flooring, drywall, and painting

Our goal is to transform your space while minimizing disruption to your daily life.

Additions

HOME ADDITIONS

When your family grows or your lifestyle changes, a home addition can provide the extra space you need without relocating.

We construct:

  • Room additions
  • Second-story additions
  • Garage additions
  • Sunrooms and enclosed patios
  • Accessory dwelling units (ADUs)

Each addition is carefully designed to integrate seamlessly with your existing structure.

Exteriors

EXTERIOR IMPROVEMENTS

The exterior of your home protects the structure while also defining its appearance. Our team provides durable and attractive exterior construction services.

Exterior improvement services include:

  • Roofing installation and replacement
  • Siding installation and repair
  • Decks and patios
  • Concrete driveways and walkways
  • Exterior painting and finishing

We use high-quality materials designed to withstand weather conditions while maintaining long-term performance.
